### First body paragraph.


#### Vocabulary for paraphrasing
Table - Chart
Shows - Illustrates/Demonstrates
Percentage - Proportion/figures/rates
Waste - Rubbish
Countries - Nations

#### Context Errors made in overview
Highest/Lowest/Larger - Correct words for percentages, proportions and numerical indicators
Trash - Trash is not academically appropriate. Waste, garbage or rubbish is the correct term
Every presented country - Across all (one, two, ..., n) countries
Throughout the years - Over the period shown
In every year - In all three years
